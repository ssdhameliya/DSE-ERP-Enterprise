Email & SMTP Setup Guidance

To dispatch invoices, receipts, and order confirmations via Google Gmail / Google Workspace, standard account passwords are blocked by Google security policy. You must generate a dedicated Google App Password.

Step-by-Step Google Setup:
  1. Navigate to your Google Account Security: myaccount.google.com/security
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is switched ON.
  3. In the search box, search for "App passwords".
  4. Give your app a name (e.g. DSE ERP Mailer) and click Create.
  5. Google displays a 16-character code. Copy this code (without spaces) and paste it into the Email App Password field.
Recommended SMTP Host:
smtp.gmail.com
Standard TLS Port:
587 (or 465 for SSL)
